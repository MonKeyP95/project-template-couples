# A suggestion card in the dispatch box — design

Date: 2026-08-03
Status: ready for a plan

## Goal

The assistant only suggests when someone presses `/ suggest`. Make it speak up
on its own: **one proactive suggestion a day, riding the daily dispatch call,
rendered as a card in the box already at the top of `/on-the-road`** — and,
unlike every card there today, **answerable**.

An answer is an approval: the couple adds the suggestion to the trip, the way
the find-a-place door adds a pick. Both the suggestion and its outcome are
stored, and the record teaches — immediately for the rest of this trip, durably
once the trip closes.

## What this changes about the dispatch box

`2026-08-03-on-the-road-dispatch-box-design.md` states the box is **not
actionable**: "No 'add to today', no apply, no write path of any kind." That
rule is deliberately superseded here, for the suggestion card only.

Everything else in that spec stands. The weather card and the dispatch items
stay read-only, keep their mandatory `window` + `sourceUrl` contract, and are
generated by the same agent under the same rules. `trip_dispatch` rows are never
written by a user action.

## What it is not

- **Not a feed.** Still one box, one day, replaced tomorrow. The suggestion is
  one card, not a list.
- **Not a second door.** The find-a-place door answers a question the couple
  asked. This card speaks first, and it does so once a day whether or not
  anyone opens anything.
- **Not a replacement for `/ suggest`.** The on-demand button keeps its exact
  current behaviour, keeps writing nothing, and is untouched by every slice
  here.
- **Not a chat.** There is no reply box. The answer is a tap.
- **Not a repeat of the deterministic nudges.** `detectNearDailyCap`,
  `detectUnloggedDays` and `detectWeatherPacking` already fire for free and
  precisely. The agent is told what they cover so it does not restate them.
- **Not about tomorrow.** Slice 1 suggests something for today, added to today's
  day. A horizon beyond today is a later decision.

## The card

Rendered in `DispatchBox` after the weather card and the dispatch items, so the
deck is at most weather + 2 items + 1 suggestion. It uses the same frame, the
same dots, the same 7-second advance, and differs only in carrying two
controls:

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| **add** | Commits the suggestion to the trip through the existing `addTodayEvent`, then marks the row `added`. |
| **×** | Marks the row `dismissed`. Nothing is written to the trip. |

Rules:

- **Answering removes the card** for the rest of the day. There is no "added!"
  confirmation state to design — the card leaves the deck and the thing it
  created appears in Today above it.
- **Interacting with the card stops the rotation for the session.** Pause on
  hover is not enough: on touch there is no hover, and a card you are mid-tap on
  must not slide out from under you. Once a control in the suggestion card is
  pressed or focused, the timer stops until the page is reloaded.
- **No suggestion is a valid day.** The agent may return nothing; then there is
  no card, exactly as a quiet dispatch day shows no items.
- **One per trip-day.** Answer it and there is nothing more until tomorrow.
- **The card renders only for a `pending` row with a non-empty title.** That one
  condition covers every case: answered, auto-dismissed empty, and no row at
  all.
- With AI off, nothing is generated and nothing renders — the existing
  `isAiEnabled()` gate in `ensureDispatch` already covers this.

## How it is produced

`ensureDispatch` keeps its one after-paint trigger, its once-per-trip-day guard
and its "never in the render path" rule, and grows a second step:

1. `dispatch` runs unchanged — `web_search_short`, up to 2 sourced items.
2. A new `road-suggestion` descriptor runs **with no search tool of its own**,
   receiving:
   - the dispatch items just found, as sourced facts it may build on;
   - the place, the date, and today's planned title + summary;
   - `profileBlock` + `tasteDirective` from `buildAssistantContext`;
   - a **history block**: the last 10 answered rows on this trip (outcome not
     `pending`), split into what was added and what was refused;
   - a line naming what the deterministic nudges already cover.
3. Both results are written in the same pass.

One web search a day, one extra cheap call, one trigger, two descriptor files
tuned independently. This is deliberately **not** one agent with two tools:
dispatch's system prompt is strict in ways a suggestion must not be (mandatory
window, mandatory source, never general news), and one prompt serving both jobs
would blunt both. It honours the `CLAUDE.md` rule that each agent is one
descriptor owning its own system prompt.

The history block ships in slice 1, not later. Without it, day two repeats day
one, and a card that repeats itself is a card nobody reads.

## The agent contract

`road-suggestion` proposes **at most one** suggestion via a forced
`propose_road_suggestion` tool:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `title` | The suggestion, short. Becomes the itinerary event's text. |
| `body` | One or two sentences on why it fits *them, today*. |
| `target` | `"event"` in slice 1. The field exists from the start so slice 2 adds values, not a schema. |
| `category` | An expense-category name for the event (`Food`, `Activities`, …), resolved against the trip's real categories at add time, as the discovery door already does. |
| `suggestedTime` | Optional `HH:MM`. Omitted when the thing has no clock. |
| `sourceUrl` | Optional. Present only when it is grounded in one of the dispatch findings — never invented. |

Prompt rules, mirroring the discipline that made dispatch work:

- **Returning nothing is correct and expected.** A full day with a good plan
  needs no suggestion. Never pad.
- **Never invent a place, price or date** that was not in the context.
- **Never repeat** something in the history block, in either direction: not what
  they added, not what they refused.
- **It must be addable.** A suggestion that cannot become a thing on today's day
  is not a suggestion in slice 1.

## Storage

One new table. Idempotent migration, RLS via `is_trip_workspace_member`, the
same shape as `trip_dispatch`:

```
trip_suggestions
  id             uuid pk
  trip_id        uuid not null references trips(id) on delete cascade
  day_date       date not null
  title          text not null default ''
  body           text not null default ''
  target         text not null default 'event'
  category       text
  suggested_time text
  source_url     text
  outcome        text not null default 'pending'   -- pending | added | dismissed
  answered_at    timestamptz
  created_at     timestamptz not null default now()

unique (trip_id, day_date)
```

The unique index is what makes "one a day" structural rather than a rule the
code has to remember — the same device `trip_dispatch` uses to make generation
happen once.

**A row is written even when the agent returns nothing**, with an empty title
and outcome `dismissed`, `answered_at` left null — nobody answered it. That is
what stops a quiet day re-generating on every load, and it is the same reasoning
as the zero-item dispatch row.

`DispatchTrigger` currently fires when there is no dispatch row. It must fire
when **either** row is missing, otherwise every trip-day already dispatched
before this ships would go without a suggestion, including the day it is first
tested on. `ensureDispatch` generates each half only if that half is missing, so
a day with a dispatch row and no suggestion row costs one cheap call and no
search.

The row's subject is a suggestion, not a trip-day, which is why this is its own
table rather than two columns on `trip_dispatch`: the summariser and any later
surface can read it without knowing the box exists, and the dispatch row stays
read-only.

## Answering

**add** calls the existing `addTodayEvent({ tripId, tripSlug, dayDate, dayId,
time, text, url, category, locationId })` — the same action the find door
commits through, unchanged — then updates the row to `added` with
`answered_at`, and refreshes. If the event write fails, the row stays `pending`
and the card stays; the outcome must never claim an add that did not happen.

**×** updates the row to `dismissed` with `answered_at`. It writes nothing else.

Both go through one server action in a new `suggestion-card-actions.ts`, guarded
by workspace membership like every other trip write.

## Learning

Two layers, deliberately different in weight.

**Live — steers now (slice 1).** `ensureDispatch` reads this trip's recent
`trip_suggestions` rows and hands the agent the added and refused lists. This is
local and immediate: it stops repetition and stops pushing what was just
refused. Nothing outside the box sees it.

**At close — teaches forever (slice 3).** `TasteSignal.kind` today is
`rated | planned | wanted | used`. A refusal becomes a fifth kind, `refused`,
gathered per trip by `gatherTripTasteSignals` and weighted by the summariser as
a negative hint. Accepted suggestions need no wiring at all: adding one creates
an itinerary event, and events already gather as `planned`.

So the durable half is one new signal kind flowing through the pipeline that
already exists — raw signals → trip summary at close → the general couple
profile. **A dismiss never writes to the profile directly.** A bad mood on a wet
Tuesday steers tomorrow and then has to survive a whole trip to change what the
app believes about them.

## Slices

1. **The card.** `road-suggestion` descriptor, `trip_suggestions` table +
   migration, generation inside `ensureDispatch`, the card with add/dismiss, the
   live history block. Target `event` only.
2. **Packing and budget targets.** The suggestion declares its own target and
   the button reads off it — "add to packing", "add to budget". New write paths
   beside `addTodayEvent`, not replacing it.
3. **Close teaches forever.** The `refused` signal kind into
   `gatherTripTasteSignals` and the summariser prompt.

## Accepted limits

- **The suggestion is cached for the day.** Anything that changes hour to hour
  (spend so far today) will be stale by evening, so the agent is told to prefer
  what is stable across a day. The deterministic cap nudge already covers the
  live spend case.
- **Silence stays ambiguous.** A `pending` row at day's end means either "never
  opened the page" or "saw it and ignored it". Only explicit answers are learned
  from; `pending` teaches nothing.
- **One suggestion a day is a low signal rate.** Accepted: the box is meant to
  stay calm, and `/ suggest` is still there for anyone who wants more.
- **Slice 1 cannot suggest the jacket.** Advice that is not addable is out until
  slice 2 lands the packing target.

## Success criteria

**Verified by Claude**

1. `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint` and `tsc --noEmit` clean.
2. The migration is re-runnable: pasting it twice leaves one table, one unique
   index, four policies.
3. `ensureDispatch` writes exactly one `trip_suggestions` row per `(trip_id,
   day_date)`; a second invocation on the same day writes no new row and makes
   no model call. On a day holding a dispatch row but no suggestion row, it
   makes the suggestion call only — no second web search.
4. An agent returning no suggestion still writes a row, with outcome
   `dismissed`.
5. `add` calls `addTodayEvent` with the suggestion's text, category and time,
   and marks the row `added` only after that action returns without an error.
6. `×` writes `dismissed` and performs no other write.
7. A non-owner workspace member can read and answer a suggestion row under RLS.
8. The history block passed to the agent contains this trip's recent added and
   refused titles, and no other trip's.
9. `trip_dispatch`, `PaceLine`, both nudges, quick expense, quick note and
   Looking Ahead are unmodified.

**Verified by the user in-app**

1. On a day with a suggestion, the box carries one more card, after weather and
   any dispatch items.
2. The card shows **add** and **×**; the read-only cards still show neither.
3. Pressing **add** puts the thing into Today, and the card disappears from the
   deck.
4. Pressing **×** makes the card disappear and adds nothing.
5. Reloading the page after answering does not bring the card back, and no
   second suggestion appears that day.
6. Touching a control stops the rotation — the box does not advance while you
   are answering, on a phone as well as a desktop.
7. The box does not change height when the suggestion card is showing, and the
   page below does not shift.
8. The suggestion is recognisably about *this* place, *this* day and *this*
   couple — not a generic destination tip.
9. On the next day, the new suggestion is not a repeat of what was added or
   refused.
10. On a quiet day the box looks exactly as it does today.
11. Readable and tappable at a phone viewport, light and dark.

## Decisions to record in `DECISIONS.md`

- The dispatch box's read-only rule is superseded for the suggestion card only,
  and why.
- Two descriptors on one trigger rather than one agent with two tools.
- `trip_suggestions` as its own table rather than columns on `trip_dispatch`.
- A dismiss reaches the couple profile only through the trip summary at close,
  never directly.
